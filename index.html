How to play

🏃 Move with the arrow keys, WASD, or the touch buttons. Reach the 🏁 flag to win each mountain.

🌲 Trees and 🪨 rocks block your path AND the coyotes. Navigate around them.

📣 Press Space or SHOUT to frighten nearby coyotes away. You have 3 hearts and 3 minutes per level. The clock pauses when the game is hidden or you return to the map.

🐺 Every successful Adventure finish adds one coyote, including replays and secret levels. Reset Coyotes returns the pack to one without losing progress; your next victory starts growing it again. Challenge Mode always has 15.

🚪 Search Mountains 5, 7 and 9 for secret doors. Find 🧰 treasure chests in every regular mountain. Running out of time starts a coyote invasion!

🔥 Challenge Mode: choose any of the 13 levels, including hidden worlds. Escape in one minute past 15 coyotes while three villagers guard the flag, leaving only one entrance. Challenge victories do not affect Adventure progress.
