How to play

🏃 Move with the arrow keys, WASD, or the touch buttons. Reach the 🏁 flag to win each mountain.

🌲 Trees and 🪨 rocks block your path AND the coyotes. Navigate around them.

📣 Press Space or SHOUT to frighten nearby coyotes away. You have 3 hearts per level.

🐺 Every new level you complete adds one coyote to every mountain, including earlier ones!

🚪 The door on Mountain 5 hides in a new square every visit, even after discovery! The 🏁 flag unlocks Mountain 6, while the secret door leads to Hollow Mountain and then Skyfire Summit.
